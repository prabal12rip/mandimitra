Perishability & Storage Guidelines

🍅 Highly Perishable (Tomato, Greens):

Shelf life ambient: 3-4 days. Decay rate ~8% per day. Cold storage (10°C) extends shelf life to 14 days.

🥔 Semi-Perishable (Onion, Potato):

Shelf life: 20-30 days in dry, well-ventilated sheds. Avoid direct rain/moisture to prevent rotting.

🌾 Durable Grains (Paddy, Ragi):

Shelf life: 6-12 months. Negligible spoilage if moisture < 12%. Safe to store for favorable price surges.
